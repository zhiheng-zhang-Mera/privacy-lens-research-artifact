I acknowledge the use of generative artificial intelligence tools in this research
project. The table below states which tools were used, for what purpose, and how
their outputs were treated. All AI-assisted material was reviewed, checked against
primary sources and edited by me; the research design, the implementation as
described, the evidence reported and the conclusions drawn are my own
responsibility.
