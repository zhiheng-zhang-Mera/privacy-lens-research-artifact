I acknowledge the use of generative artificial intelligence tools in this research
project. The table below states which tools were used, for what purpose, and how
their outputs were treated. All AI-assisted material was reviewed, checked against
primary sources and edited by me; the research design, the evidence reported and the
conclusions drawn are my own responsibility.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.16\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.30\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.46\textwidth}}
\toprule
Tool & Purpose of use & How the output was treated \\
\midrule
Gemini & Discussion of the research proposal; locating the specific provisions of the GDPR relevant to the design & Used to orient the literature and to find candidate provisions. Every provision was then read in the official EUR-Lex text and cited from that source; no legal conclusion in this thesis rests on an AI summary. \\
ChatGPT & Batch acquisition of the Android applications used as the evaluation corpus and driving them through the test scripts; assistance with patches to the application source & Used to script the bulk download of applications, to feed them into the existing test scripts, and to propose code changes. The corpus definition, the acceptance criteria, the test assertions and the interpretation of the results are my own; every proposed change was reviewed before it was kept, and the executed code is archived in this repository so that a reader can inspect exactly what was run. \\
DeepSeek & Repository and file operations; natural-language assistance with version control & Used as a tooling assistant for staging, committing and recording changes. It did not author research content; every change was reviewed before it was recorded. \\
Grammarly & Language checking of the written thesis & Used for grammar, spelling and punctuation only. Suggested edits were accepted or rejected at my discretion, with no change to technical claims, numbers or citations. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
